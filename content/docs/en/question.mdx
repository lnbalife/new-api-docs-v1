## Q: Why am I getting a 401 Unauthorized error?

A: Please ensure that your sk-... key is correct and that your account has remaining quota.


## Q: What is the token limit for a single request?

A: This depends on the selected model. NodeKey adheres to the original limits set by each upstream provider.

## Q: Does NodeKey retain my data?

A: No. We only act as a relay service and do not store prompts or completion data, retaining only the metadata needed for billing.
